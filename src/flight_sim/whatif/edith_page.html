EDITH: many simulated flights, and what happens in them

EDITH is the team's Monte Carlo simulation. Instead of flying the rocket once, it flies it hundreds of times. Each flight has the wind, the weather, the motor's strength and the launch rail set a little differently, the way they differ from one real launch day to the next. Counting what happens across all the flights gives the chance of each outcome. This page shows those chances, where the rocket is likely to peak and land, and which IREC rules it might break.
